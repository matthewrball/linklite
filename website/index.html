How it works

Three displays.
Nothing extra running.

LinkLite creates one virtual display per dock monitor, reads changed areas with ScreenCaptureKit, and sends the pixels straight to the dock. No backend. No login-window agent.

One display per monitor

Native 4K60 on the tested dock. Changed 64×16 tiles are sent again; settled areas are not re-encoded every frame.

The dock draws the pointer

Moving the mouse sends a few bytes of position data, not another full-screen encode.

Your pixels stay local

Screen Recording reads only the virtual displays. Captured pixels go over USB, not to a server. No telemetry.

An honest benchmark

Less memory.
More CPU.

134 MB versus 513 MB on two 4K monitors. Under motion, the whole GPU was 15% busy versus 33%.

DisplayLink Manager encodes on the GPU. LinkLite encodes on the CPU and used about 3.4 cores under full-window motion, against 20% for Manager.
